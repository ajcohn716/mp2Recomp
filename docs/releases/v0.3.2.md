# mp2Recomp v0.3.2

Up to four players can now use their own SDL-compatible controllers on one
computer. Start the game normally and check the controller assignments under
**F1 → Input**. The keyboard also works for Player 1. Controllers can be
connected during play, and unplugging one leaves the other players on their
assigned ports.

Download and extract the archive for your platform. To upgrade, copy
`userdata/` from your previous installation to bring over saves, settings,
and the cached ROM. For online play, everyone in the room should update to
v0.3.2.

The Windows build is unsigned. Smart App Control may block it on some Windows
11 PCs.
